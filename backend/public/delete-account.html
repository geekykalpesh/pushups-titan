DATA SAFETY & ACCOUNT DELETION

Pushup Titan

Request Account & Associated Data Deletion • Published by GeekyKalpesh

1. Overview of Account Deletion

In accordance with Google Play Store Developer Policies and global privacy regulations (GDPR and CCPA), Pushup Titan provides users with complete autonomy over their data. You have the right to request the permanent deletion of your account and all associated telemetry records at any time.

2. What Data Is Deleted?

When your account deletion is processed, the following data is permanently purged:

  • Account Credentials: Your username, email address, password hash, and security PIN hash.
  • Workout Telemetry: All recorded pushup counts, workout timestamps, sets, rep velocity, and personal records.
  • Nutritional Records: All egg logs, calculated protein metrics, and historical nutrition targets.
  • Habit Data: All streak counters, calendar activity heatmaps, and progress charts.

No telemetry or personal identifiers are retained on our servers after deletion.

3. How to Request Account Deletion

You can delete your account using either of the two methods below:

Method A: Directly Inside the Pushup Titan Mobile App

  1. Open the Pushup Titan app on your Android device.
  2. Navigate to the Profile tab from the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Scroll down to the Account Settings section.
  4. Tap Delete Account & Data and confirm your request.

Method B: Via Email (No App Installation Required)

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot access your device, submit a deletion request directly to our developer team:

Send an email to: geekykalpesh@gmail.com

Subject: Account Deletion Request - Pushup Titan

Body: Please include the registered email address used for your Pushup Titan account.

Email Deletion Request

4. Processing Time & Data Retention

  • In-app deletion: Accounts deleted via the mobile app are processed immediately.
  • Email requests: Requests sent via email are verified and permanently purged within 48 hours (maximum 30 days under GDPR).
  • Data Retention: We do not retain backup copies of deleted user accounts or personal fitness telemetry.

OFFICIAL PRIVACY POLICY

Pushup Titan

Effective Date: November 8, 2026 • Published by GeekyKalpesh

1. Introduction & Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how Pushup Titan, operated and authored by GeekyKalpesh ("we", "us", or "our"), collects, uses, and safeguards information when you use our mobile application and backend services.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any personally identifiable information you provide through the app.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: When you register, we collect your email address, username, and encrypted password. If enabled, your 4-digit quick passcode is also salted and securely hashed using bcrypt.
  • Fitness & Activity Data: Workout logs (pushup repetitions, timestamp, sets, notes, personal records) and daily progress goals.
  • Nutritional Information: Egg consumption logs, calculated protein metrics, and daily nutrition targets.
  • Device & Network Data: Standard connection headers and crash prevention telemetry strictly necessary for communicating with our API. We do NOT collect GPS locations or sensitive contacts.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To power your personal daily analytics, habit streak calendars, and GitHub-style activity heatmaps.
  • To synchronize workout logs securely across your devices.
  • To notify you of scheduled reminders or workout alarms you configure.
  • No Selling of Data: We never sell, lease, or monetize your data with third-party advertisers or data brokers.

4. Data Security & Storage

All network communications between the mobile application and our backend server are encrypted using industry-standard SSL/TLS protocols. User authentication utilizes JSON Web Tokens (JWT) and secure one-way salted bcrypt hashing. Database records are maintained on enterprise-grade PostgreSQL cloud infrastructure (Neon Cloud) protected by strict firewalls and security controls.

5. User Rights & Data Deletion

Under Google Play Store Developer Guidelines and applicable data protection regulations (GDPR / CCPA):

  • You may view, edit, or delete individual workout and egg records directly inside the application at any time.
  • Account Deletion: You have the right to request permanent deletion of your account and all associated fitness logs. To initiate account deletion, contact us at geekykalpesh@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request". All records will be permanently purged within 30 days.

6. Health & Medical Disclaimer

Pushup Titan is an exercise log and habit tracker created for informational and motivation purposes only. It is not a medical device, nor does it provide clinical health advice. Consult a healthcare professional before initiating any intense exercise or nutrition regimen.

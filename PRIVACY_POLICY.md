## 1. Introduction
This Privacy Policy outlines how **Pushup Titan**, created and managed by **GeekyKalpesh** ("we", "us", or "our"), collects, processes, and protects your information when you use our mobile application and related API services.

We respect your personal privacy and are dedicated to ensuring transparent data protection compliant with Google Play Developer policies, GDPR, and CCPA.

---

## 2. Information Collected
We collect only the minimum data required to deliver core workout and nutrition habit tracking capabilities:
- **Account Details:** Email address, username, and an irreversibly hashed password (bcrypt). Optional 4-digit quick passcode is also salted and hashed.
- **Fitness Records:** Pushup counts, workout session dates/times, personal bests, and streak logs.
- **Nutritional Fuel:** Egg counts, protein estimates, and user-defined target benchmarks.
- **Diagnostics:** Standard HTTPS connection metadata strictly for reliable synchronization and error prevention. We do **not** collect GPS tracking, contacts, or sensitive hardware identifiers.

---

## 3. How We Use Your Data
- Synchronize your progress securely across your devices.
- Compute daily goals, progress rings, and habit heatmaps.
- Trigger workout reminders and alarms you configure.
- **We NEVER sell, rent, or distribute personal user information to third-party advertisers or data brokers.**

---

## 4. Security & Cloud Architecture
Data is encrypted in transit using SSL/TLS protocols and stored securely on cloud-managed PostgreSQL infrastructure (Neon Cloud) with encrypted connection pooling and firewall isolation.

---

## 5. Account and Data Deletion
Under Google Play Store policies:
- Users can delete individual workout and egg records directly inside the app.
- Users can request complete and permanent deletion of their account and all stored history at any time by sending an email to [geekykalpesh@gmail.com](mailto:geekykalpesh@gmail.com) with the subject *"Account Deletion Request"*. All associated personal data will be completely erased from the database within 30 days.

---

## 6. Health & Fitness Disclaimer
Pushup Titan is designed solely as a fitness log and motivation tool. It does not provide medical diagnosis or clinical treatment. Consult your physician prior to undertaking any rigorous physical training.
